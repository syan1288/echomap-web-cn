Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Echo Map helps you create illustrated map memories from places, buildings, photos, notes, and audio. This policy explains what information the service may process, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address and, when supplied by your sign-in provider, your name and profile image. For Google sign-in, Echo Map uses basic account information to create and secure your account; it does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google services.
  • Your memories: building or place names, prompts and mood text, dates, coordinates or place labels, photos, generated images, notes, and audio you choose to add.
  • Location information: if you grant browser/device permission, the app may use your current location to center the map or place a memory. If precise location is unavailable, the app may use an approximate location derived from your IP address. The app may also read location/date metadata from a photo you select. Echo Map does not use continuous background location tracking.
  • Orders and credits: plan selected, price, credit amount, order status, and payment-provider transaction references needed to confirm purchases and maintain your balance. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider, not stored by Echo Map.
  • Technical information: browser/device and request information may be processed by hosting, authentication, storage, map, and security providers to operate and protect the service.
  • Browser storage: some preferences, sign-in state, and unsynced app data may be stored locally on your device. Clearing browser/app storage can remove local-only data.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide sign-in, save and sync your memories, display maps and places, generate or edit images, manage usage limits and purchases, respond to support requests, maintain security, and improve reliability.

When you request image generation, the selected image, building name, and prompt may be sent through Echo Map’s server to the image-generation provider configured for that request. Depending on the model selected and service configuration, providers may include Google Gemini or Google Cloud Vertex AI, Volcengine’s Jimeng, or another provider made available in the app.

3. Cloud storage and service providers

If you sign in and use cloud features, account and memory data may be stored with Supabase, which provides authentication, database, and private file storage. Echo Map applies account-based access controls to cloud memories; do not upload information you are not comfortable storing or processing through the service.

To provide maps, place search, and geocoding, the app may send relevant search text or coordinates to the map/location provider configured for your version, such as Mapbox, Amap, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, CARTO, or an IP-location provider. Hosting and payment providers (for example, Vercel and the payment option shown at checkout) process information needed to deliver their services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer.

4. Google user data

When you choose “Sign up with Google,” Google shares the basic identity information needed for authentication, such as your email address and, if available, your name and profile image. Echo Map uses this data to create or access your account and provide account-related features. We do not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build unrelated profiles.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep account and cloud-memory information while needed to provide the service. You can delete individual cloud memories in the app where that feature is available. To request account or associated cloud-data deletion, contact us from an address associated with your account so we can verify the request. Some transaction, security, or backup records may be retained for a limited period where needed for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or recovery purposes.

Data stored only in your browser can usually be removed by clearing the browser/app’s site data. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete cloud data; contact us to request cloud deletion.

6. Your choices and security

You can decline location or microphone permissions, choose whether to use Google sign-in, remove memories, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. You can also revoke Echo Map’s Google account access in your Google Account settings.

We use reasonable safeguards, including authenticated access controls for cloud content. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children and changes

Echo Map is not designed for children under 13, or the minimum age required in your location. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact s.yan88212@gmail.com or s.yan12@outlook.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月21日

Echo Map 帮助你把地点、建筑、照片、文字和录音整理成地图记忆。本政策说明服务可能处理哪些信息、使用目的以及你可以采取的选择。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 账户信息：邮箱，以及登录服务提供的姓名和头像。使用 Google 登录时，Echo Map 使用基本账户信息创建和保护账户；不会请求访问 Gmail、云端硬盘、联系人或其他 Google 服务。
  • 记忆内容：你添加的建筑或地点名称、提示词和心情文字、日期、坐标或地点名称、照片、生成图片、备注和录音。
  • 位置信息：在你允许浏览器或设备定位后，应用可能使用当前位置来定位地图或放置记忆。无法取得精确位置时，应用可能根据 IP 地址估算大致位置。应用也可能读取你选择的照片中的地点或拍摄日期元数据。Echo Map 不进行持续的后台定位。
  • 订单与额度：为确认购买和维护余额，处理套餐、金额、订单状态、额度及支付服务商交易编号等信息。支付凭据由支付服务商处理，Echo Map 不存储。
  • 技术信息：托管、认证、存储、地图和安全服务商可能处理浏览器/设备及请求信息，用于运行和保护服务。
  • 浏览器存储：部分偏好、登录状态和未同步数据可能保存在设备本地。清理浏览器或应用数据可能删除仅保存在本地的内容。

2. 信息用途

我们使用信息提供登录、记忆保存与同步、地图和地点显示、图片生成或编辑、额度与购买管理、客服、安全保护和可靠性改进。

当你请求生成图片时，你选择的图片、建筑名称和提示词可能经 Echo Map 服务器发送给该次请求所使用的图像生成服务商。依据你选择的模型和服务配置，服务商可能包括 Google Gemini 或 Google Cloud Vertex AI、火山引擎即梦，或应用中提供的其他服务商。

3. 云端存储和服务商

登录并使用云端功能时，账户和记忆数据可能保存在提供认证、数据库和私有文件存储的 Supabase。Echo Map 对云端记忆设置账户访问控制；请勿上传你不愿通过本服务存储或处理的信息。

为提供地图、地点搜索和地理编码，应用可能向当前版本配置的地图/位置服务商发送相关搜索文字或坐标，例如 Mapbox、高德地图、OpenStreetMap/Nominatim、CARTO 或 IP 定位服务商。托管和支付服务商（例如 Vercel 及结账时显示的支付方式）会处理提供相应服务所需的信息。

上述服务商依据各自的条款和隐私声明处理信息。我们不出售个人信息。法律要求、保护用户或服务安全，或发生业务转让时，我们可能依法披露相关信息。

4. Google 用户数据

选择“使用 Google 注册”时，Google 会提供认证所需的基本身份信息，例如邮箱，以及可用时的姓名和头像。Echo Map 使用这些信息创建或访问你的账户并提供账户功能；不会将 Google 用户数据用于广告、出售，或建立与服务无关的个人画像。

5. 保存期限与删除

我们会在提供服务所需期间保存账户和云端记忆。应用提供相应功能时，你可以删除单条云端记忆。如需删除账户或相关云端数据，请通过与你账户关联的邮箱联系我们，以便核实请求。法律、会计、防欺诈或备份恢复所需的部分交易、安全或备份记录可能在有限期间内保留。

仅存于浏览器的数据通常可通过清理浏览器/应用的网站数据删除。删除应用不一定会删除云端数据；如需删除云端数据，请联系我们。

6. 你的选择与信息安全

你可以拒绝定位或麦克风权限、选择是否使用 Google 登录、删除记忆，并在适用情况下联系我们申请访问、更正或删除信息。你也可以在 Google 账户设置中撤销 Echo Map 的访问权限。

我们采取合理的安全措施，包括对云端内容进行登录访问控制。但任何互联网传输或存储系统都无法保证绝对安全。

7. 儿童与政策更新

Echo Map 并非为未满 13 周岁或你所在地规定的最低年龄以下儿童设计。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们。

服务变化时我们可能更新本政策，并在此发布修订版本及更新日期。
